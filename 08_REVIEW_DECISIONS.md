1. **Study priority:** Should the approved agricultural-model benchmark be completed first, or may the bounded greenhouse evidence/LLM-instrument comparison be accepted instead? Please specify one primary deliverable.
2. **Science and scoring:** Which reference case, original source passages, validation variables, model versions and expert raters are approved? Can 46 recovered questions form a documented pilot or must missing items be added to reach 50?
3. **Engineering direction:** Is a deterministic, source-gated rule system with optional LLM explanation an appropriate technical comparator? What outcome must it improve to justify complexity?
4. **Administration and continuation:** What attendance/remote-work activities count toward official hours, what is the formal closeout requirement and is continuation beyond Dec 1 feasible under approved procedures?
